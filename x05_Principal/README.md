# The Principal Hacker's Path: The Crossroads (2025 Edition)
**Target Decision:** Choose Your Path - Founder | Stay Technical/Researcher | Executive Leadership (CISO/Head of Security) | **Salary Range:** $300k - $500k+ | **Timeline:** Ongoing

## 🎯 The Crossroads: Choose Your Path

You've reached Principal. You're making $180k+. You're a technical god. You've discovered zero-days. You've shaped the industry.

**But here's the brutal truth they don't tell you:**

**Principal is a CROSSROADS. Not a destination.**

You're standing at a fork in the road. Two paths diverge:

**Path 1: Executive Leadership**  
You report to CISO, CTO, VP of Engineering. You build RFPs. You improve products. You lead teams. You do interviews. You handle the "boring shit." Less hands-on hacking. More strategy, budgets, politics, and business.

**Path 2: Stay Technical / Become Founder**  
You keep your head down. You stay technical. You invest. You build. You become a founder. You keep doing the fun stuff. Less politics. More hacking. More freedom.

**There is no wrong choice. But you MUST choose.**

## The Principal Reality Check

**Here's what they don't tell you:**

**Principals don't just hack. You're BUSINESS LEADERS.**

You're building RFPs. You're managing budgets. You're leading teams. You're conducting interviews. You're improving products. You're handling the "boring shit."

**But here's the thing:**

**If you don't want that path, you don't have to take it.**

You can:
- **Stay Principal** - Keep your head down, stay technical
- **Become Founder** - Build your own thing, get equity
- **Invest** - Build wealth, financial freedom
- **Stay Technical** - Keep hacking, avoid politics

**There is no wrong choice. But you MUST choose.**

**The brutal truth:** Principal is a crossroads. You can go executive (more responsibility, less hacking) or stay technical (more freedom, less politics). Both are valid. Both are rewarding. But they're DIFFERENT.

**Choose wisely. Choose intentionally. Choose YOUR path.**
